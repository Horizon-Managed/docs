The full [v1.14.x platform](/changelog/2026-07-01) is now generally available. Here's everything that shipped this week, grouped by area with links to the detailed release notes.

## New features

### Service desk & automation

- **Ticket queue** — triage helpdesk tickets from a sticky command region with SLA stat tiles and inline actions. See [Ticket queue](/helpdesk/ticket-queue).
- **Ticket Peek** — a two-pane slide-out for working a single ticket, with live SLA, asset, RMM posture, Nexie summary, and remote actions. See [Ticket Peek](/helpdesk/ticket-peek).
- **Workflow studio** — no-code automation rules that fire on ticket events (10 triggers). See [Ticket arrival routing](/workflow-studio/ticket-arrival-routing).
- **Knowledge base**, **Dispatch board**, and the field-tech **Mobile app**. See the [Monday recap](/changelog/2026-07-02).

### Sales, quotes & billing

- **CRM** with a 360° account view, weighted deal pipeline, activity log, and AI lead capture from sales-call transcripts. See [CRM](/crm/index).
- **Discovery blueprint** — Nexie-generated Client Operations Blueprint that converts to a draft quote in one click. See [Discovery blueprint](/assessment/index).
- **Quotes & invoices** — sectioned versioned quotes with online e-sign, one-click conversion to invoices, and AR aging. See [Quotes & invoices](/billing).
- **Contracts & e-sign**, **Purchase orders & Pax8 procurement**, and the **Product catalog**. See the [mid-week ship](/changelog/2026-07-02-quotes-billing).

### Projects & field

- **Projects** with WBS, earned-value job costing, and client-signed change orders.
- **Work orders** for field crews.
- **Time tracking** with QuickBooks push.
- **Team & employees** record.

See [Projects & field](/changelog/2026-07-03-projects-field).

### Construction & bid

- **Bid engine** — AI-assisted takeoff for construction, low-voltage, and electrical bids.
- **Drawings** — versioned site drawings with 3D floor-plan analysis.
- **Field survey** — iPad pre-bid walkthroughs that convert straight to a bid.

See [Construction & bid](/changelog/2026-07-03-construction-bid).

### RMM & infrastructure

- **NexusRMM Go agent** for Windows, macOS, and Linux. See [Deploy the RMM agent](/rmm/deploy-agent).
- **Remote sessions & recording** — in-browser terminal, remote desktop, and SOCKS tunnels with auditable WebM recording.
- **Device vault** with encrypted credentials and **FortiGate automation**.
- **Infrastructure verification** and **host & service health** dashboards.

See [RMM & infrastructure](/changelog/2026-07-03-rmm-infrastructure).

### Security & SIEM

- **SIEM** — auto-detecting syslog listener (TLS/plain on port 6514), PCAP parsing, and Nexie AI threat analysis.
- **Email security** — automated Microsoft 365 phishing triage.
- **Vulnerability scanning** with a Red Team wizard.
- **Security posture dashboard** — single-pane rollup.

See [Security & SIEM](/changelog/2026-07-03-security-siem).

### Compliance & governance

- **Compliance Composer** — one engine covering 15 frameworks with auto-collected evidence and Nexie gap analysis.
- **CMMC Level 2 workspace**, **NCSR intake**, **Risk acceptance registry**, and the **Live Review Engine** for signed client attestations.

See [Compliance & governance](/changelog/2026-07-03-compliance-governance).

### Client portal & onboarding

- **Client portal** — tenant-scoped self-service for tickets, invoices, quotes, contracts, KB, and risk acceptances, with an embedded Nexie concierge.
- **Client onboarding** — 9-phase, 30-day pipeline from signed contract to steady-state managed services.

See [Client portal & onboarding](/changelog/2026-07-03-portal-onboarding).

### AI & analytics

- **Nexie assistant** — conversational AI over your live tenant data (read-only, row-limited, cost-reported).
- **NexusPulse (SMART Numbers)** — MSP KPI engine.
- **Employee productivity analytics** — consent-gated per-employee reporting.

See [AI & analytics](/changelog/2026-07-03-ai-analytics).

### Procurement & network

- **Distributor catalog (Pax8)** — unified search with cost/MSRP comparison across every billing-term combination. See [Distributor catalog](/distributors/catalog).

## Known limitations

Each detail entry lists the specific limitations for its area. The most notable carry-overs:

- **Distributor catalog** — Pax8 descriptions render as escaped text; **Add to quote** is disabled pending quote integration.
- **RMM agent** — auditable session recording on macOS/Linux is a stub; field auto-update is Windows-only.
- **Dispatch board** — Microsoft 365 calendar sync is one-way (NEXOS CORE → Outlook).
- **AI features** — Nexie, AI lead capture, Discovery blueprint, and KB article drafting require a tenant Claude API key.

See the individual entries above for the full list.
