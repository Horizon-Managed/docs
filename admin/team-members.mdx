Everyone on your team signs in to NEXOS CORE as a **user** in your tenant. You
add users from **Settings → Users**, either by **inviting** them (they receive
an email and set their own password) or by **creating** the account directly
with a password you set. Each user is assigned a [role](/admin/roles-permissions)
that governs what they can see and do.

## When to use it

- Onboard a new technician, dispatcher, or manager.
- Send someone a self-service invite so they choose their own password.
- Create a shared or service account directly, without email.
- Re-send or cancel an invitation that hasn't been accepted yet.

## Two ways to add someone

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Invite Team Member" icon="envelope">
    Enter their name, email, and role. They receive an email with a secure link
    to set their own password, then sign in. **No password is set by you** —
    best for real people onboarding themselves.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add New User" icon="user-plus">
    Enter their name, email, role, **and a password**. The account is usable
    immediately and **no email is sent** — useful for a shared or service
    account, or when you're setting someone up in person.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Inviting a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Users">
    Click **Invite Team Member**. (The separate **Add New User** button creates
    an account with a password instead and sends no email.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Provide the person's **email**, **name**, and the **role** to assign. The
    invite form has no password field — the invitee sets their own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They receive an email">
    NEXOS CORE emails the invitee a **"You're Invited"** message with an
    **Accept Invitation** button. The invitation is valid for **7 days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They set a password and sign in">
    The link opens a set-password page showing the account it's for. Once they
    choose a password, they're taken to sign in — and appear in your Users list
    as an active member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The invitation email is sent from **your own configured email address** when
  you've connected one under **Settings → Integrations**. If your tenant hasn't
  set up email yet, NEXOS CORE sends the invite from a Horizon system address so
  onboarding is never blocked — connect your own sender when you're ready and
  future invites come from your domain automatically.
</Note>

## Managing pending invitations

Invitations that haven't been accepted appear in the Users screen with two
actions:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Resend** | Issues a fresh link and emails it again — use if the original expired or was missed. |
| **Cancel** | Revokes the invitation so its link can no longer be used. |

## Good to know

- **Invitations expire after 7 days.** Use **Resend** to issue a new link.
- An invited user chooses their **own** password — you never see or set it. The
  link is single-use and tied to that one invitation.
- **Add New User** is the only path that sets a password on the user's behalf,
  and it sends no email — share those credentials securely and out of band.
- The assigned **role** takes effect when the user first signs in
  (see [Roles & permissions](/admin/roles-permissions)).
- You can't invite an email that already belongs to a user in your tenant.
