---

Your clients sign in to the Client Portal at `/portal/login` with an **email
address and a password**. Each client can additionally turn on **two-factor
authentication** using any authenticator app, entirely self-service — you don't
have to set it up for them.

This is separate from staff sign-in. Portal users are *contacts*, they
authenticate through their own stack, and nothing here affects how your team
signs in to NEXOS CORE. See [Sign-in & MFA](/admin/sign-in-mfa) for the staff side.

## When to use it

- A client signs in day to day with their email and password.
- A client wants a second factor on their account and enrolls an authenticator app.
- A client has lost their phone and needs to get in with a recovery code.
- A client forgot their password and needs a reset link.
- A new client sets their password for the first time from an invite.

## First sign-in: setting a password

When you invite a contact to the portal they receive an email with a link to
choose their own password. The same flow handles resets. Passwords must meet the
platform policy — at least 12 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase
letter, a digit, and a symbol — and are stored bcrypt-hashed.

Setting a password from an invite or reset link signs the client straight in,
because the link already proved they control the mailbox.

## Signing in

Email plus password. A few deliberate behaviours worth knowing when a client calls
you about sign-in:

- **Wrong email, wrong password, and a disabled account all return the same
  message.** That's intentional — it stops the form being used to discover which
  of your clients have portal access.
- **After 5 consecutive failures the account locks for 15 minutes.** There's also a
  per-network rate limit, so a shared office IP is handled more forgivingly than a
  single account.
- **A client who has no password yet is told so** and is emailed a link to set one,
  rather than being left on a form they can't satisfy.

## Two-factor authentication

A client turns 2FA on themselves from **Profile → Two-Factor Authentication**:

1. They choose **Set up two-factor**.
2. They scan the QR code with any authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator,
   Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy…), or type the key in by hand.
3. They save **ten single-use recovery codes**, shown once.
4. They enter a current 6-digit code to switch it on.

From then on, signing in asks for a code after the password. The profile panel
shows how many recovery codes remain and warns when they're running low.

<Note>
  The QR code is generated by NEXOS CORE itself and embedded directly in the page.
  The shared secret behind it is never sent to an outside image service.
</Note>

### If a client loses their authenticator

They enter one of their **recovery codes** in the same box as the 6-digit code.
Each code works exactly once.

<Warning>
  Recovery codes are currently the **only** self-service way back in. If a client
  loses their authenticator and has used or misplaced all ten codes, there is no
  button in the staff app to clear their second factor yet — it needs a support
  request to NEXOS CORE. Make sure clients actually save their codes when they
  enroll. A staff-side reset is planned.
</Warning>

### Turning 2FA off

The client needs **both** their current password and a current code from their
authenticator. That way neither a borrowed laptop with a live session nor a stolen
password is enough on its own to remove the second factor.

## Password resets don't skip 2FA

If a client has 2FA switched on and then resets their password, they still have to
enter a code before they get in. Mailbox access is one factor, not two — otherwise
anyone who could read a client's email could work around the second factor and
enrolling in it would be pointless.

## What gets recorded

Every sign-in event on a client's account is written to the portal audit log with
the IP address and browser: successful sign-ins, failed passwords, lockouts, 2FA
prompts, 2FA successes and failures, recovery-code use, and 2FA being switched on
or off (including refused attempts to switch it off).

## Good to know

- **2FA is per-client and optional.** There's no tenant-wide switch to require it
  of every portal user yet.
- **The one-time email link is no longer a way to sign in.** It now only carries
  invites and password resets.
- Invite and reset emails need a configured mailer or email account, the same as
  before.
