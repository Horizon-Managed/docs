Releases v1.66.0 to v1.101.0, from September 13 to September 22. This month
brings project billing from start to finish, a mobile loop that takes a ticket
from raised to billed, and much clearer reporting on integrations and AI spend.
Role permissions are now enforced everywhere, so read the warning below.

## Security and permissions

<Warning>
  Role permissions now control access to tickets, companies, quotes, invoices,
  contracts, projects, purchase orders, compliance and the knowledge base. Your
  existing tenant received the standard set of permissions for its built-in
  roles. If you created custom roles, check that they hold the permissions their
  users need.
</Warning>

- **Roles & Permissions explains itself.** Every staff and client-portal
  permission now has a written definition: what it allows, what it doesn't, and
  where it applies. Wildcard permissions show what they cover. See
  [Roles & permissions](/admin/roles-permissions).
- **Role and user administration needs permission.** Creating, editing or
  deleting roles, and managing users, now needs its own permission. Nobody can
  grant a permission they don't hold. A system role's permissions can't be
  rewritten, and every change is written to the security audit log.
- **Client portal permissions mean what they say.** One portal permission was
  renamed to say it views a profile rather than edits one, and one permission
  that did nothing was retired. A portal user without permission to create
  tickets or reply no longer sees **New Ticket** or a reply box.
- **MFA setup stays inside NEXOS CORE.** The QR code for staff MFA enrolment is
  now generated by NEXOS CORE itself.
- **No third-party scripts.** NEXOS CORE no longer loads any script or
  stylesheet from outside servers, including on the pages where your clients
  sign contracts and change requests.
- **Quotes can't be approved twice.** A double-click or a reopened approval link
  could record a second approval and bill the client again. A repeat now just
  reports that the approval is already recorded.

## Helpdesk

- **The full ticket page shows everything Peek does**: related tickets, the
  money position (contract, coverage, rate, hours, labour and materials), the
  effort summary and real Suggested KB results. It also uses the full width of
  your screen.
- **Ticket charges.** The **Time & Billing** tab can now add, edit and remove a
  charge. Each charge shows what it will bill before you save it, and it
  invoices at its marked-up amount.
- **Resolve really resolves.** Peek's **Resolve** button drafts a resolution for
  you to review and edit, then saves it and closes the ticket out. A resolution
  you wrote yourself is no longer logged as time against Nexie.
- **SLA displays agree.** Peek's resolution row reads the resolution clock,
  tickets answered on time show **Met** (or **Met late** with the overrun), and
  the resolve SLA bars count down live between page loads on the queue, the full
  ticket and Peek.
- **Your statuses everywhere.** When you stop a timer, the status picker lists
  the statuses from **Settings → Helpdesk**, including ones you created.
- **Loop Health** (Helpdesk → Loop Health) shows how quickly tickets go from
  raised to billed over the last 30 days, split by where the work happened.
- **Mailbox problems no longer repeat all day.** When Microsoft refuses a
  helpdesk mailbox's credential, polling for that mailbox pauses for six hours
  and resumes by itself once the credential works again.
- **Only real people appear.** A sample ticket conversation with made-up people
  has been removed. Job titles beside messages now come from the author's own
  profile or contact record.
- The ITIL **Problems**, **Changes**, **CMDB** and **Service Catalog** pages:
  their links, filters and **+ New** buttons work, and Problems no longer fails
  on a problem with no impact or urgency.

## Mobile

- **Create a ticket from your phone**, with big-button pickers for type and
  priority. See [Mobile](/mobile/index).
- **Close & Bill** logs the remaining time and resolves the ticket in one step.
- **Draft from notes** writes a first draft of the work description from the
  ticket's notes, on the phone and on the desktop. You review and edit it before
  logging.
- **Time Capture** offers every kind of work you can time: tickets, work orders,
  project tasks, projects, deals and surveys. The bottom bar gains **Sales**.
- **Phone time bills.** Time stopped on a phone now bills like time stopped on a
  desktop, and the Time Capture page loads again.

## Time and billing

- **Log Time asks for a work type and billing role**, then shows the contract,
  rate and amount before you save. Time you log by hand bills again. Hours with
  no rate are flagged as unpriced instead of disappearing from the invoice.
- **Every approved quote gets its contract.** If contract creation is
  interrupted, a recovery check creates the missing contract within the hour.
- **Turn a deal into a quote.** A **+ Quote** action on a deal in the Sales
  Workspace pre-fills the quote and keeps the link between the deal and the
  quote.
- **QuickBooks schedules are checked.** Each sync confirms that every contract's
  QuickBooks recurring template still exists and is active. A missing or
  deactivated template is reported in the Sync Log.

## Projects

- **Invoice a project from its page.** A fixed-price project bills its budget
  items, and a time-and-materials project bills its billable hours. Approved
  billable expenses are included either way. A completed fixed-price project
  prompts you to raise its final invoice. See [Projects](/project/index).
- **A new Milestones tab.** Completing a billing milestone raises its draft
  invoice, and milestone-billed projects can no longer be billed in full by
  mistake.
- **Change orders and expenses bill on their own invoices.** A client-signed
  change order can raise its own invoice. Approved expenses can be billed
  together, and expenses can now be approved or rejected from the Costs tab.
- **From contract to project.** A signed contract can create the project that
  delivers it, from the contract page. Delivery contracts get a delivery
  project. Recurring contracts get an onboarding project only for their one-time
  fees.
- **Work orders invoice.** A workflow can now raise a draft invoice when a work
  order is completed.

## Purchase orders

- **Receiving works.** **Receive** opens a screen with each line's ordered,
  received and outstanding quantities, so you can record partial deliveries. The
  sidebar's **Receiving** link lists every order still waiting on delivery.
- **QuickBooks status is visible.** Each purchase order shows whether it reached
  QuickBooks and, if not, why. Approving no longer waits for QuickBooks to
  answer.
- **A full audit trail.** Approvals, rejections and receipts are recorded on the
  order's audit trail. See [Purchase orders](/po/index).

## Integrations

- **Sync Log** (Operations → Sync Log) lists every QuickBooks, billing,
  distributor and Hudu result in one place, failures first, with repeats
  collapsed. Each entry links to the record that needs fixing. You can dismiss
  entries, or permanently delete them with a signed confirmation.
- **An Integrations page** gives each scheduled integration its own on/off
  switch. It shows what the integration calls, how often, and when it last ran
  and succeeded. Turning one off asks first and is audited.
- **Pax8 credentials live in NEXOS CORE.** Each distributor has a card under
  **Settings → MCP**. You can add, verify and remove its credentials, and NEXOS
  CORE talks to Pax8 directly once they are verified. Errors now give the
  vendor's own reason. See [Distributor catalog](/distributors/catalog).
- **Hudu assets show addresses.** They carry each device's IP and MAC addresses
  and the date they were collected. See [Hudu](/integrations/hudu).

## AI

- **A spending limit for AI.** Each tenant has an enforced ceiling, daily and
  monthly. The default is $25 a day and $250 a month, and the client portal
  assistant has its own budget. You get an alert at 80% and again when a limit
  is reached.
- **An AI Spend page** breaks down the month by client, user, model, feature
  and day, and the pricing behind it is now accurate. Clients see their own
  usage in the portal, but never your cost.

## Other improvements

- **My Profile saves.** You can change your own name and password. Changing your
  password signs you out on your other devices.
- **Dispatch invites use the client's time zone.** Calendar invites are created
  in the time zone of the client site, not always Eastern.
- **Compliance evidence is collected.** **Collect Evidence** in CMMC and the
  Compliance Composer gathers evidence for each client, and device compliance
  scores are filled from what your agents report.
- **The deal panel shows real data.** The Sales Workspace shows each deal's own
  contact and its real stage, in place of placeholder people and stages.
- Avatar initials display correctly for names that start with an accented
  letter, a non-Latin character or an emoji.
