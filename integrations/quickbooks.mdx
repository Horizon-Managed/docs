NEXOS CORE connects to your QuickBooks Online company so QuickBooks stays the
single source of truth for financial data. After a one-time OAuth connection,
NEXOS CORE periodically **pulls** your products, customers, employees, invoices,
and payments into a local mirror (for fast in-app display) and **pushes**
estimates, invoices, purchase orders, and approved timesheets out to QuickBooks.
Each data type flows in one direction.

## When to use it

- Bill from NEXOS CORE while keeping QuickBooks authoritative for accounting.
- Push approved quotes to QBO as estimates, and quotes/time/contracts as invoices.
- Bill recurring contracts through QuickBooks recurring templates, created from
  each contract's **QuickBooks Recurrence** card.
- Push approved timesheets to QBO as time activities.
- View synced invoices, payments, and products in NEXOS CORE without hitting the
  QBO API on every page.

## Connecting

Connect under **Settings → QuickBooks** — a standard OAuth authorization to Intuit
(per-tenant client credentials, with a switch between your sandbox and production
company). Tokens refresh automatically; a revoked token marks the connection
inactive.

## Write lock

When you connect your real (production) QuickBooks company, **writes start
locked**. NEXOS CORE reads your books and links clients to their QuickBooks
customers, but writes nothing to them: no invoices, payments, recurring templates,
customers, estimates, purchase orders or time. Anything that would have written
says so plainly instead ("QuickBooks is read-only for this company…"), and nothing
is sent.

To start billing from NEXOS CORE, an admin clicks **Turn on writes** on
**Settings → QuickBooks**. NEXOS CORE asks first and names the company. **Lock
writes** turns them off again. Every change is recorded in the security audit
log with who made it and when, and the card shows the last change.

- The **sandbox** company is writable, so testing keeps working.
- Switching a company to **production** always locks writes again, even if they
  were on in the sandbox.
- Reading is never locked. Refreshing an invoice, looking up a customer and
  syncing keep working while writes are locked.

## What syncs which way

**In (QuickBooks → NEXOS CORE):** company info, products/services, customers (to
companies), employees, invoices with line items, and payments — upserted into the
local mirror. Invoices and payments pull incrementally after the first full sync.

**Out (NEXOS CORE → QuickBooks):** estimates from quotes; invoices from time
entries, tickets, quotes, and contracts; recurring templates from contracts;
purchase orders; and time activities from approved timesheets. Nothing goes out
while writes are locked (see **Write lock**).

**Cadence:** a full sync runs every ~15 minutes, plus a manual **Sync Now**.
Recurring invoices are raised by QuickBooks from each contract's recurring
template, not by NEXOS CORE.

## Good to know

- **It's one-way per data type, not a live two-way sync.** An invoice you create
  pushes out and is mirrored back on the next sync, but later edits in NEXOS CORE
  don't automatically re-push.
- Invoice sync **skips** any QuickBooks invoice whose customer isn't yet mapped to
  a NEXOS CORE company.
- Product sync brings in Service and Non-Inventory items (not Inventory/Group or
  QBO system items).
- Timesheet push needs each user mapped to a QuickBooks employee first (in
  Settings) — unmapped users are skipped.
