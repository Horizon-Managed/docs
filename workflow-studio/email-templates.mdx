Email templates are the emails your workflow rules send with the **Send Email** action. You edit them in one editor, which you can open from two places:

- **Helpdesk** → **Email Templates**.
- **Orchestrator** → open a module → the **Email Templates** tab.

Both places edit the same templates.

## The editor

Each template has a name, a **Subject** and a body. The body has two tabs:

- **Design** shows the email the way your client gets it, and you can type straight into it.
- **HTML** shows the same email as code.

The two tabs edit the same body. A change in one shows up in the other.

The toolbar works in both tabs: **B**, **I**, **U**, **H2**, **P**, **List**, **Link**, **Divider** and **Image**. In **Design** it formats what you've selected. In **HTML** it wraps what you've selected in the matching tag.

**Link** opens a field for the address. You can put a variable there too, such as `{{ticket_portal_link}}`.

Saving a template changes only what you edited. It stays active, and it keeps its type.

## Variables

A variable is a placeholder, like `{{contact_first_name}}`, that fills in from the ticket when the email goes out.

The **Variables** panel beside the editor lists every variable you can use. Each one shows its code, what it holds, and its value on your most recently updated ticket.

To add a variable:

1. Click where you want it, in **Subject**, **Design** or **HTML**.
2. Double-click the variable in the panel, or select it and press **Enter**.

Search the panel by name, code or example value.

In **Design**, a variable shows as a highlighted chip with its value filled in from that ticket. You can't type inside a chip, so it can't be broken by accident. A chip with a dashed underline has no value on that ticket. Tick **Show variable codes** to see the codes instead of the values.

<Note>
A value from a ticket always arrives as plain text. A ticket titled `<b>Urgent</b>`, or one that mentions another variable, appears exactly as typed. It can't change your email's formatting or pull in other values.
</Note>

How values are filled in:

- **Dates and times** are in your time zone, the one set on your default business calendar.
- **Status, priority, type, source and team** show their names as you see them in NEXOS CORE (**In Progress**, not `in_progress`).
- **`{{tech_name}}`** reads **Support Team** when the ticket has no technician.
- **`{{company_name}}`** falls back to your own company name when the ticket has no client.
- **`{{note_body}}`** is the newest note the client can see. It is never an internal note.
- A variable with no value on the ticket is left out of the email.
- A code that isn't on the list below is left in the email exactly as written, so you can spot the typo.

Templates written with older spellings still work. The **Also accepted** column lists them.

## Images

Click **Image** in the toolbar to add a picture. The image goes in where your cursor is.

- Only `.avif`, `.webp`, `.png` and `.jpg` files can be added.
- Each image can be **500 KB** at most.
- A file whose contents don't match its extension is refused, even if it's renamed.

The editor tells you why when it refuses an image.

To change an image in **Design**, click it. Then:

- **Resize it:** drag its corner handle (it keeps its proportions), type a width in pixels, or choose **Small**, **Half** or **Full width**.
- **Align it:** choose **Left**, **Center** or **Right**.
- **Remove it:** choose **Remove**.

The size is saved in the form both Outlook and Gmail follow, and an image is never wider than the email.

Your client's email app loads each image from a private link that can't be guessed. Only someone who has the email can see the image. Every company's images are kept separate.

<Note>
Images can't be deleted once added. An email you've already sent keeps showing them.
</Note>

## Brand header and footer

Your **Brand Settings** hold one header and footer for all your emails: your logo, company name, colours, support email and footer text. To edit them, open **Orchestrator** → **Email Templates** → **Brand Settings**.

When a template's **Use brand header & footer** switch is on, the email goes out wrapped in your Brand Settings. The switch is in the editor, beside the **Design** and **HTML** tabs, and it's on for new templates.

Turn it off for a template that already has its own full design: its own banner, header or footer. Otherwise that email would show two headers.

If you haven't saved any Brand Settings, emails go out without a header or footer, whichever way the switch is set.
