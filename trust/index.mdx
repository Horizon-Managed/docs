NEXOS CORE holds sensitive information: customer contact rosters,
signed contracts, ticket histories, credentials for remote monitoring
agents, and — through the SIEM and email-security modules — evidence
from active security incidents. Trust is not a marketing claim on this
platform; it's a stack of specific technical guarantees you can
inspect.

This section documents those guarantees. Each page is written for
three audiences: your security team who need engineering-level detail,
your auditors who need control-to-evidence traceability, and your
prospects who just need to know it's safe.

## Compliance frameworks

NEXOS CORE maps its security controls to the frameworks our customers
are audited against — SOC 2, CMMC, HIPAA, PCI, NIST 800-53, CIS, ISO
27001, GDPR, CCPA, FedRAMP, GLBA, FERPA, and SOX. Evidence for each
control (implementation code, proof tests, verification dates) is
maintained alongside the code and refreshed on every change. Contact
your Horizon Managed Services account contact for the current
evidence package.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue in NEXOS CORE, please
email [security@horizonmanaged.com](mailto:security@horizonmanaged.com)
with a description and (if possible) reproduction steps. We
acknowledge within one business day and coordinate disclosure with
you.
