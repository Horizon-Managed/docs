Your deal **pipeline** is topped by two rows of key performance indicators (KPIs) that turn the cards on the board into numbers you can act on. The first row summarizes the *open* pipeline and your recent close performance. The second row shows *won* revenue by time period, each with a chip telling you whether you are up or down against the prior period.

Open it at `/crm/pipeline`. The KPI rows sit above the drag-and-drop stage columns and recalculate every time the page loads, so they always reflect the live state of your deals.

## When to use it

- Start your sales stand-up on the pipeline page and read the two KPI rows aloud — they answer "how are we doing?" without a report.
- Check **Weighted Forecast** when you need a realistic revenue number to commit to, rather than the raw sum of every open deal.
- Watch **Win Rate** and **Avg Days to Close** over time to spot a stalling or improving sales motion.
- Review the **Closed $** row at the end of a week, month, or quarter to see what actually landed and whether you beat the last period.
- Use the quarter toggle to compare Q1–Q4 performance across the current year.

## The summary row

The top row has six cells describing your open pipeline and recent close rate.

| Cell | What it means | How it is calculated |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Pipeline** | The full dollar value of every open deal on the board. | Sum of each open deal's value. |
| **Weighted Forecast** | A probability-adjusted forecast — your most realistic number. Shown in the brand accent color as the hero figure. | Sum of each deal's value × that deal's own win probability. A $10,000 deal with a 40% win probability contributes $4,000. |
| **Active Deals** | How many open deals are in the pipeline. | Count of deals on the board. |
| **Avg Deal Size** | The typical size of an open deal. | Total Pipeline ÷ Active Deals. |
| **Win Rate** | The share of recently closed deals you won. | Won ÷ (Won + Lost) over the **last 90 days**, shown as a percentage. |
| **Avg Days to Close** | Your recent sales-cycle length. | Average of (close date − created date), in whole days, over deals **won in the last 90 days**. |

<Note>
**Win Rate** and **Avg Days to Close** only count deals closed in the **last 90 days**, so they describe your *current* sales motion rather than all-time history. Until you have recent closes, each cell shows a dash (`—`) with "no closes yet" or "no won deals yet" instead of a misleading `0%` or `0 days`. Win Rate needs at least one won or lost deal in the window; Avg Days to Close needs at least one won deal.
</Note>

### How win probability drives the weighted forecast

Weighted Forecast is driven by the **Win Probability (%)** you set on each individual deal, not by the deal's stage. Every deal carries its own win-probability field (you enter it on the deal form, and it shows as a percentage pill on the deal card). The forecast is the sum of each deal's value multiplied by that deal's own probability, and it updates on the next page load. Moving a card between stages does **not** change its win probability — the number stays whatever you set on the deal until you edit it. Keep each deal's probability honest and the weighted number stays trustworthy.

## The Closed $ row

The second row reports **won** revenue by close date. Only deals in a won stage with a recorded close date are counted — open and lost deals never appear here. Each cell shows the dollar amount (abbreviated, with the full figure on hover), the number of deals that closed in that window, and a delta chip comparing it to the prior like-period.

- **This Week** — won value since the start of the current week, versus the previous week.
- **This Month** — won value since the start of the current month, versus the previous month.
- **Quarter** — won value for a single quarter of the current year, versus the preceding quarter. This cell defaults to the quarter in progress (labeled **CURRENT**) and has a **Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4** toggle so you can switch between quarters. Q1's comparison uses last year's Q4 as its baseline.
- **Year to date** — won value since January 1, versus the same span of the prior year (year-start through today, one year ago).

### Reading the delta chips

Each Closed $ cell carries a small "vs prior like-period" chip:

- An **up arrow with a percentage** — this period beat the prior one, by that percent.
- A **down arrow with a percentage** — this period trailed the prior one.
- **new** — you closed revenue this period after closing nothing in the prior one, so there is no percentage to compute.
- **No chip** — both this period and the prior one were zero.

The arrow direction is purely factual (higher or lower), independent of any color theme.

## How closing a deal feeds these numbers

The KPIs are driven entirely by how deals close on the board, so no separate data entry is needed.

<Steps>
<Step title="Drag a deal into a Won or Lost stage">
On the pipeline board, drag a deal card from its current column into a stage marked as won or lost.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm the close">
Because this closes the deal, NEXOS CORE asks you to confirm: **"Move this deal to '<stage>'? This closes the deal."** Confirm to proceed, or cancel to snap the card back to where it was. Reordering cards *within* a column — including a column that is already won or lost — saves silently with no prompt.
</Step>
<Step title="The close date is stamped">
Confirming stamps the deal's close date. Won deals immediately feed **Win Rate**, **Avg Days to Close**, and the matching **Closed $** cells; lost deals feed **Win Rate** only.
</Step>
<Step title="Reload to see the KPIs move">
The KPI rows recompute on page load, so refresh the pipeline to see the updated numbers.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Everything is tenant-scoped.** The KPIs only ever count your own organization's deals.
- **Only won deals count as revenue.** The Closed $ row and Avg Days to Close ignore lost deals; lost deals affect Win Rate only.
- **The 90-day window is fixed** for Win Rate and Avg Days to Close. It is not adjustable from the page.
- **Time windows follow the server clock.** Week, month, quarter, and year boundaries are computed server-side, so the periods are consistent for everyone in your organization.

## How it connects

These KPIs read directly from your [CRM deal pipeline](/crm/index) — the same board where you manage stages and drag deals through their lifecycle. Each deal's Win Probability (%), which powers the Weighted Forecast, is set on the deal itself.
