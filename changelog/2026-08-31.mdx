Releases v1.53.0 to v1.65.2, from August 24 to August 27. Most of the work went
into the Orchestrator and into contract signing and billing. One change can
affect workflows you have already built, so read the warning below first.

## Orchestrator

<Warning>
  The action and trigger lists in the workflow builder used to include more than
  a hundred actions and triggers that did nothing when a workflow ran. They are
  gone. If one of your workflows used one, that step was already doing nothing.
  It now no longer appears. Review your workflows for steps that have
  disappeared, and rebuild them with an action that runs.
</Warning>

- **The builder offers only what runs.** Every action and trigger in the
  dropdowns now has something behind it. Four modules (Security, Infrastructure,
  Nexie and Procurement) now show an empty action list, because none of the
  actions they offered ever ran.
- **New triggers.** Six events that already happened in practice can now start a
  workflow: a client email reply, an inbound email opening a ticket, the two
  email-security screening outcomes, a note being added, and a ticket being
  resolved. **Contract fully signed** is a trigger too.
- **Actions that were hidden are now selectable**: **Apply Contract Policies**,
  **Invoice from Contract** and **Promote Company to Customer**. A new **Send
  Billing Schedule to QuickBooks** action sets up a contract's recurring
  schedule in QuickBooks. If a workflow fires twice, the contract still gets one
  schedule.
- **See what a workflow does.** Selecting a workflow shows its trigger and each
  action in order, with a plain description of every step. If the workflow has
  run, you also see what each step did last time: applied, correctly skipped, or
  failed.
- **A new layout.** The Workflows tab is now a studio: a workflow list on the
  left, the flow in the centre, and the workflow's details and last-run results
  on the right. The Orchestrator hub now uses the same card layout as the IQ launcher.
- **The run log is honest.** It now tells applied, skipped and failed steps
  apart instead of recording success for all three.
- **Custom actions are removed.** The custom-action form saved actions that
  never ran. No tenant had created one.
- The builder now displays correctly in the tinted and parchment themes, and the
  Orchestrator link in the sidebar always opens the hub.

## Contracts and signing

- **Signing a contract no longer bills the client by itself.** Billing is a
  decision you make with the **Generate Invoice** button on the contract.
  **Generate Invoice** also no longer double-bills: on a contract that already
  has an invoice, it opens that invoice instead.
- **Contracts with several documents attached** now need a signature on every
  document before they activate.
- **The public signing page shows the whole contract**: the pricing table, the
  attached documents and the signatures already collected. If any of these
  cannot load, the page stops instead of asking the client to sign an
  incomplete contract.
- **Signatures are embedded in the signed document.** Previously, signing could
  report success even though the signature image never reached the file.

## Billing and QuickBooks

- **Create in QuickBooks** on a contract sets up its recurring billing schedule
  in QuickBooks, and QuickBooks raises each invoice. Pressing it again never
  creates a second schedule. One-time contracts are told the schedule doesn't
  apply.
- **No double billing.** A contract that QuickBooks bills on a schedule refuses
  **Generate Invoice**. The contract page shows which system owns the schedule.
- **Invoices from a quote work again.** Generating an invoice from an approved
  quote failed and copied no lines. It now copies every accepted line, with its
  product and the quantity the client approved.
- **Invoices remember their source.** An invoice raised in QuickBooks from a
  quote, a recurring contract or billable ticket time now links back to that
  quote, contract or time.

## Sales Workspace

- **Time a deal.** You can start the timer from the deal you're working on.
  Clicking another deal pauses the first deal's timer and starts or resumes the
  one you opened, so each deal gets its own time entries.
- **New Lead** no longer rejects a Next Step you have already typed, and
  **Analyze with Nexie** no longer clears fields it has no value for.

## Time and timesheets

- **Only one timer runs at a time.** Starting a timer on a ticket that already
  had one used to leave another timer running, so both were billed.
- **Timesheet approval needs permission.** Approving or rejecting a timesheet
  needs the approve-timesheets permission. People without it no longer see the
  **Approve** and **Reject** buttons.
- **You submit your own timesheets.** You can submit only your own timesheet,
  unless you can approve timesheets. That lets payroll close out an absent
  employee's week.

## Bug fixes

- Confirmation dialogs no longer show a second browser dialog on top of the
  styled one.
- The workflow goal strip's figures are centred in their cells.
