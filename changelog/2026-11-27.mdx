Releases v1.116.0 to v1.123.0, from November 2 to November 5. This month
protects your QuickBooks books, adds the first security-tool integration, and
connects devices and tickets more closely. Three changes alter default behavior,
so read the warnings below.

## QuickBooks and billing

<Warning>
  **Production QuickBooks companies are read-only until you allow writes.**
  Connected to a production QuickBooks company, NEXOS CORE reads it and links
  clients to its customers, but writes nothing: no invoices, payments, recurring
  templates, customers, estimates, purchase orders or time. An admin turns
  writes on under **Settings → QuickBooks**. Until then, anything that would
  write says that QuickBooks is read-only for this company, and nothing is sent.
  Sandbox companies stay writable. Switching a company to production always
  locks it again, and turning writes on or off is recorded in the security audit
  log.
</Warning>

- **Invoices show where they came from.** An invoice's page names the
  **Contract** it bills and the **Quote** that contract was sold on, each as a
  link. Invoices brought in from QuickBooks list every line, on screen and in
  the printable copy.
- **Payments say what they paid.** A client's **Payments** panel has a **For**
  column listing the contract, project and tickets behind each invoice a payment
  was applied to. Payments mirrored from QuickBooks carry their reference number
  and payment method. Refreshing an invoice brings in its QuickBooks payments
  straight away.
- **Template invoices link to their contract**, whether QuickBooks' schedule
  raised them or someone pressed **Use** in QuickBooks. The contract's billing
  history shows them.
- **Bill the first month.** QuickBooks never raises a scheduled invoice for a
  date that has already passed. The **QuickBooks Recurrence** card therefore
  offers **Bill first month** and holds **Create in QuickBooks** until that
  month is billed. Billing the first month late keeps the contract's billing
  day.
- **Payment terms carry over.** A contract's recurring template uses the
  contract's payment terms instead of the customer's default. If the QuickBooks
  company doesn't have the matching term, you're told which term to add.
- **The QuickBooks Recurrence card** can link the client to a QuickBooks
  customer right there. It is the contract's only billing action when QuickBooks
  bills the contract, so **Generate Invoice** no longer appears next to it.

## Security tools

- **Connect Huntress** from **Settings → Integrations → Security tools**. Your
  API key and secret are stored encrypted. **Save & verify** shows which account
  the key belongs to and lists your Huntress organizations.
- **Suggested client links.** Each unlinked Huntress organization shows the
  client it most likely belongs to, with the reasons: the same Microsoft 365
  tenant, an unambiguous name, or a name the client already uses in QuickBooks
  or a distributor. Nothing links until a person clicks **Link**. Ambiguous
  matches are never suggested, and **Review suggestions** confirms the strong
  ones in one pass. Every link is audited.
- **Microsoft 365 tenant lookup.** To find a client's Microsoft 365 tenant from
  its domains, NEXOS CORE asks Microsoft's public sign-in discovery service. No
  credentials are involved. You can switch this off per tenant on the
  Integrations page.

## RMM and devices

<Warning>
  **Tickets raised by RMM alerts no longer email the device's contact.**
  Workflow emails to the client (ticket created, updated, resolved, closed, SLA
  and alert cleared) skip these tickets, and the run log records why.
  Technicians are still notified, and replies a technician writes still send. To
  email clients about these tickets, turn on **Email the client about tickets
  raised by RMM alerts**, next to **Raise tickets from RMM alerts**.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Remote access has its own permission.** **Remote into devices** now controls
  remote desktop and terminal from the device page, the Devices drawer, a ticket
  and Peek, as well as recorded support sessions started from a ticket. Admin,
  manager and technician roles hold it by default. Other roles no longer have
  remote access unless you grant it. A second permission, **Edit device notes
  and custom fields**, is held by admins and managers, and you can grant it to
  technicians. Moving a device to another client also needs device write
  permission. See [Roles & permissions](/admin/roles-permissions).
</Warning>

- **Manage custom fields.** **RMM › Configure › Custom fields** lets you define,
  rename, change and delete device custom fields. You can see how many devices
  hold a value for each field and who last changed it. Tenant admins can edit;
  everyone else can view.
- **Tickets and devices link both ways.** The device named on a ticket or in
  Peek opens that device's page. The ticket queue can be filtered to one device.
  On a device page, the open-ticket count includes every open ticket, and **+N
  more** opens the queue filtered to that device.
- **Device tickets get a contact.** A ticket raised for a device with no contact
  named now takes the device's assigned contact, if that contact belongs to the
  ticket's client. Moving a device to another client clears a contact who
  doesn't belong to the new client.
- **Device counts agree everywhere.** The home Dashboard, the RMM Dashboard and
  the Devices list count devices the same way. A device that has never checked
  in is shown as **Never checked in**, not as Offline.
- **The device page matches the helpdesk.** A device's Contract and SLA now come
  from the same rules the helpdesk applies to its tickets. An alert with a
  ticket shows the ticket's priority and where that priority came from.
- **Your check-in interval reaches devices.** The interval set under **Settings →
  Tenant** is now sent to each device and applied. A device's feature toggles
  show what the agent actually applied.
- **Saved views follow one set of rules** on the Devices list, the product
  catalog and the ticket queue. Only the person who shared a view, or a tenant
  admin, can delete it.
- **More ticket sources in rules.** The Orchestrator's **Source** condition
  lists every source a ticket can have, including RMM, EPA and SIEM.
- **Safer device commands.** The device page can queue only actions the agent
  understands, and an action that needs an agent feature isn't sent to an agent
  that lacks it. Every queued command records who requested it.

## Bug fixes

- Confirm dialogs work with a mouse again. Since late October, clicking
  **Confirm** closed the dialog without acting. This affected **Get approval
  link**, **Send Quote** and every other "Are you sure?" prompt.
- **Review Document Before Signing** on a contract shows the document again,
  including PDF previews.
- The QuickBooks customer picker's search icon no longer fills the whole sheet.
- Pages no longer log about twenty console errors on load.
