The Security Posture Dashboard is a single-pane security overview for a client.
It rolls up scores from three sources — compliance frameworks, vulnerability
scanning, and RMM endpoint-agent health — into one **Overall Security Score
(0–100)**, with per-category breakdowns, endpoint stats, and a recent-alerts
feed. It's a **read-only** view: it presents scores and alerts already produced
by the other modules; it doesn't run scans or compute the underlying scores
itself.

Open it at `/security/dashboard`.

## When to use it

- Get an at-a-glance security posture for a client without opening the compliance,
  vuln-scan, and RMM tools separately.
- Track the single Overall Security Score as a headline health metric.
- Spot which category — compliance, vulnerability, endpoint, or identity — is
  dragging the score down.
- Check how many monitored endpoints are online and their policy-adherence rate.
- Triage the most recent security alerts and update their status.

## What it shows

- **Overall Security Score** — a rollup across all contributing sources.
- **Category scores** — compliance, vulnerability, endpoint, and identity.
- **Endpoint stats** — total vs. online agents and the resulting compliance rate.
- **Recent alerts** — the latest alerts by severity, source, and status; you can
  update an alert's status inline.

## Good to know

- It's a **read-only aggregation** — the scores come from the
  compliance, vulnerability-scanning, and [RMM](/rmm/deploy-agent)
  modules. Fix a low score at its source, not here.
- The Overall Score is a pooled ratio across sources, so a source with a large
  maximum weighs more heavily — read it alongside the category breakdown.
