Releases v1.102.0 to v1.115.0, from October 12 to October 29. This is the
biggest month so far. Every invoice your client receives now goes through
QuickBooks first, project billing was rebuilt from start to finish, and the
ticket and RMM screens were redesigned. Several changes alter how billing works
day to day, so read the warnings below.

## Billing and QuickBooks

<Warning>
  **Recurring billing now comes only from QuickBooks.** NEXOS CORE's own hourly
  recurring-billing run is gone. QuickBooks recurring templates do all recurring
  billing. A live contract with no QuickBooks template says **Not billing: no
  QuickBooks recurring template** on its page. The **Recurring contract
  billing** switch has been removed from Integrations.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **QuickBooks no longer decides which client a customer belongs to.** The
  QuickBooks sync used to link customers to clients by name, create a client for
  every customer it couldn't match, and overwrite clients' email and phone. It
  now only refreshes the list of QuickBooks customers. A person links each
  client to its QuickBooks customer, and every link is audited.
</Warning>

- **QuickBooks sends your invoices.** Push a draft invoice with **Push to
  QuickBooks**, then use **Send from QuickBooks** to email it. NEXOS CORE no
  longer emails invoices itself. Invoices show **In QuickBooks — not sent**
  until QuickBooks sends them. The invoice list adds a **Not sent** filter and
  bulk send. See [Quotes & invoices](/billing/index).
- **QuickBooks' figures win.** An invoice in QuickBooks shows QuickBooks'
  subtotal, tax, total and balance, and can only be edited in QuickBooks.
  **Record Payment** posts the payment to QuickBooks first. Credit memos are now
  issued in QuickBooks: they appear on the invoice they were applied to, and
  **Issue Credit** has been removed.
- **Voiding undoes the billing.** Voiding an invoice voids it in QuickBooks
  first and frees the milestone, change order, expenses or hours it billed so
  they can be billed again. A draft that never reached QuickBooks can be
  discarded.
- **Ready to bill** (Billing → Ready to bill) is billing's queue across every
  client: completed project milestones, final invoices, signed change orders,
  approved expenses, time-and-materials hours and drafts that never reached
  QuickBooks. Rows that can't be billed yet say why and link to the fix. Select
  rows and press **Create in QuickBooks**, which previews every invoice first.
  The queue is also on your phone, under More.
- **Ticket time is reviewed before it bills.** A support admin approves each
  closed ticket's billable time. They can trim the hours that bill, exclude an
  entry with a reason, classify unclassified work, send time back to the
  technician, and word the line the client will read. Approved time is invoiced
  from Ready to bill as one line per ticket, with no hours or rates on the
  client's invoice.
- **Covered work never bills.** If the client's contract covers a kind of work,
  that time can't be marked billable from Log Time or the timer, and an invoice
  that would bill it is refused.
- **Link clients to QuickBooks customers.** New Client checks QuickBooks for
  matching customers before the client is created. You can link one, or create
  the client in both NEXOS CORE and QuickBooks. Every client page shows which
  QuickBooks customer it bills. A customer that QuickBooks still bills can't be
  moved or unlinked. See [QuickBooks](/integrations/quickbooks).
- **A Finance tab on every client** mirrors QuickBooks: open balance, overdue
  amount, unapplied credit, open invoices, payments and credit memos.
- **Sandbox or production, per tenant.** Choose your QuickBooks environment
  under **Settings → QuickBooks**, with a go-live readiness checklist. Switching
  environments and connecting QuickBooks are admin-only.
- **Recurring templates bill the right lines.** A contract's QuickBooks template
  bills its recurring lines only, so one-time setup fees are no longer billed
  every month. A recurring line on a different cadence from the contract is
  refused when the contract is saved or sent.
- **Contract versions take over billing.** When a new version is signed after a
  seat change, it replaces the old version's QuickBooks template instead of
  billing alongside it, and distributor subscriptions move to the new version.
- **Default contracts.** You can mark a client's default service-desk contract
  on the contract page. New tickets use the default. If no default is set and
  the client has exactly one contract in force, they use that contract;
  otherwise the ticket is Time & Materials. A default that has expired or been
  cancelled is ignored.
- **Products and timesheets stay classified.** QuickBooks product sync no longer
  marks every Service item as a monthly charge. Approved timesheet hours go to
  QuickBooks as not-billable time, so they can't be invoiced twice. Survey time
  is never billable.

## Projects

<Warning>
  **Only people with billing access create project invoices.** Completing a
  milestone no longer raises an invoice by itself: it marks the milestone
  **Ready to bill**, and billing raises the invoice. The same applies to change
  orders, expenses and final invoices. Nobody can approve an expense they
  entered.
</Warning>

- **A Billing tab on every project** answers "what is left to bill on this
  job?". It shows the contract value, change orders, the amount in QuickBooks,
  the amount ready to bill and the remainder. Below that is one row per billable
  item, with its state and any blocker. A **How this project bills** card on the
  project page summarises the method and lists what must be in place before
  billing can reach QuickBooks.
- **New projects ask how they bill**: fixed price, milestones,
  time-and-materials (with an optional not-to-exceed), or not billable. A
  milestone schedule must add up to the price. You can create a project that
  delivers a signed contract from the form or from the contract, with a preview
  of exactly what will be created.
- **One budget.** Budget lines carry a client price beside their cost, and every
  tab shows the same two figures. Fixed-price invoices bill the client price,
  never the cost. Once the final invoice exists, the budget lines lock.
- **Change orders are priced from lines** (labour, materials and fixed fees),
  with a running price, expected cost and margin. You decide when each one
  bills: on approval, with a milestone, or at completion. The client sees prices
  only, never your cost. On the signing page clients can approve, decline with a
  reason, or ask a question. You can edit, withdraw, resend or copy the link for
  a change order, raise one from your phone, and hand your phone to the client
  to sign.
- **Milestones** can be entered as an amount or a percentage of the price, show
  how many days late they are, and ask you to confirm before completing, showing
  exactly what becomes ready to bill.
- **Time-and-materials projects bill month after month.** Each invoice takes the
  approved hours not yet billed, up to a date you choose, with one line per task
  and role. No hour is billed twice.
- **Expenses and materials.** Expenses carry a markup and a receipt photo or PDF,
  and you can log them from your phone. Materials received on a purchase order
  marked billable to the client are billed on the project's next charges
  invoice.
- **Project margin follows what the project bills.** Job Costing and
  Profitability now forecast revenue from the fixed price, milestones or priced
  hours, and show QuickBooks' own invoiced figures.
- **Tasks are an outline.** Drag to reorder and nest tasks, and use Tab and
  Shift+Tab to indent and outdent. WBS numbers come from each task's position,
  and parent tasks roll up hours, dates and progress. Tasks can be assigned and
  timed from the desktop.
- Project tabs now save what you enter, including stakeholders, risks and
  change requests. The sidebar link to the project status board is now labelled
  **Project Board**.

## Helpdesk

- **The ticket page is rebuilt.** One chronological stream replaces the
  Conversation and Activity tabs, with tabs to filter notes, changes, time,
  automation and sessions. **Actions ▸ Edit ticket** edits the title, status,
  priority, team, assignee, type, source, contact, contract and company. You can
  add a contact or link a device after a ticket is created. A brief strip at the
  top shows both SLA legs, age, effort, value and device posture.
- **Value tells covered from billable.** A ticket's value shows total labour,
  with the billable amount and the amount the contract covered beneath it.
- **The ticket queue sorts on every column.** Contact has its own column,
  Priority is a column again, the column header stays in place while you scroll,
  and every column can be resized. The Breaching, Unowned and Healthy lane
  headings are gone, so your sort applies to the whole list. See
  [Ticket queue](/helpdesk/ticket-queue).
- **An SLA runway** replaces the queue's stat tiles. It places every open ticket
  by the time left until its next deadline, with counts for past due, due within
  the hour, due in one to two hours, paused and no SLA. Each count opens a
  filtered queue.
- **Pending pauses the SLA clock.** Time a ticket spends in Pending, or in any
  status you've marked as pausing the SLA, is added back to its deadline. Paused
  tickets show **Paused** with the time left. The SLA clock stops when a ticket
  is resolved or closed, and a ticket resolved on time shows **✓ Met**.
- **Honest sources.** Tickets a technician creates are recorded as **Manual**
  and show **TECH**. Onboarding and email security tickets show **SYSTEM** and
  **SECURITY** instead of **PORTAL**. The unsupported **Chat** source has been
  removed. Onboarding phases and email security escalations open their tickets
  again.
- **Nexie (Automation)** is now the author shown for notes and changes made by
  automation, instead of "Unknown" or "System".
- **Related tickets** say why they're related (same device or similar title)
  and which device they're on.
- **Dashboard tiles** for SLA breaches, online agents, companies and security
  score now show real numbers.
- Peek's keyboard shortcuts no longer fire while you're typing a reply.

## RMM

- **The RMM menu is reorganised** into Fleet (Dashboard, Devices, Discovered),
  Operate (Remote sessions) and Configure (Deploy, Device Registry). Agents is
  now **Devices**, and Recordings is now **Remote sessions**. Old links still
  work.
- **A new Devices list** has a fleet health strip, search by hostname, user, IP,
  MAC or serial, filters, a column chooser, saved views, a side drawer with
  remote actions, and bulk ticket creation. See [Devices](/rmm/devices).
- **The device page is rebuilt.** It has a status header and toolbar, six
  headline figures, and the business context: company, contract, contact, SLA
  and open tickets. Eight tabs cover Overview, Alerts and Activity, Software,
  Patches, Hardware, Network, Security, and Services and Processes. Devices also
  have **notes** and typed **custom fields**. See
  [Device details](/rmm/device-details).
- **The RMM Dashboard shows fleet metrics** in place of the agent table, with a
  client picker. See [RMM dashboard](/rmm/dashboard).
- **Alerts can raise tickets.** Turn on **Raise tickets from RMM alerts** under
  **Settings → General → Agent Staleness Detection**. A server that goes offline
  then opens a ticket with the right client, priority and team. If the device
  comes back before anyone works the ticket, the ticket resolves itself with a
  plain-English note. Outages are capped at 20 tickets per rule per client per
  hour. See [Alert tickets](/helpdesk/alert-tickets).
- **Deploy by company.** Every company gets its enrolment token when it is
  created. The Deploy page shows each company's download, share link and install
  command directly. The Windows install command now installs correctly. See
  [Deploy agent](/rmm/deploy-agent).
- **Better agents.** Agents report their version and real CPU, memory and disk
  figures, and Windows software inventory lists names, versions and publishers
  correctly. On Windows, the agent service restarts after a crash. Uninstalling
  an agent removes it cleanly.
- **Security hardening.** Each agent can only report for itself, and remote
  sessions open only on your organization's devices. Remotely uninstalling an
  application handles application names safely.

## Products, distributors and quotes

- **The product catalog scales.** It pages, sorts and filters on the server,
  with saved views, a quick look per product, a price-book health strip and bulk
  edit for vendor, category and active status. A product can be **Priced per
  quote**, and products can be billed per site, domain or organization. See
  [Product catalog](/product/index).
- **Distributors moved to Settings → Integrations.** Each distributor has a card
  showing what it powers, its sync status and its largest problems. Each sync
  run has its own page with errors grouped by problem and a CSV download.
  **Match clients** links Pax8 and Sherweb companies to your clients.
- **Sherweb connects directly** with your partner API credentials and syncs
  subscriptions nightly. Distributor catalogs now refresh weekly, and
  subscriptions still sync every night.
- **The client Subscriptions tab** groups subscriptions by distributor, with
  monthly cost, seats, renewals and anything no contract bills. **Link to
  contract** writes the catalog product and catalog price, never the
  distributor's cost.
- **Get approval link** on a draft quote shows a link you can share with the
  client yourself, without sending an email. Picking a client company fills in
  its primary contact.
- **Dictate a quote.** The Nexie Quote Builder has a microphone. In Chrome and
  Edge, the audio is sent to the browser vendor to be transcribed; Safari
  transcribes it on the device.

## Bug fixes

- Names sort alphabetically regardless of capital letters, in every list and
  dropdown.
- Creating and editing contracts works again after the "priced per quote"
  change.
- Filter menus and the "Save this view" dialog no longer pop open on their own
  after the page updates.
- Sheets and dialogs behave as focused modals: Escape closes them, and focus
  stays inside until they close.
- After a deploy, a normal reload always loads the new styles.
- Forms that save in the background no longer also reload the page underneath
  you.
- Changing a ticket's customer and choosing the new customer's contact in the
  same save no longer fails.
