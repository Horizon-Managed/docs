Every [alignment review](/composer/alignment-reviews) generates a client-facing
**Executive Summary** — a single, branded, printable NEXOS CORE document that turns
the review into the deliverable you hand the client. It's assembled entirely from
what the review already knows, so there's nothing to re-enter. Open it at
`/summit/reviews/{id}/executive-summary`.

The report **prints or saves to PDF straight from the browser** (Print → Save as
PDF) on a clean, US-Letter light-paper theme that isolates the deliverable from the
app chrome.

## What's on it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Alignment score & maturity">
    The review's alignment score as a score ring and a maturity gauge, with the gap
    to target called out.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Key findings">
    The misaligned controls — not-aligned and at-risk — ordered most-urgent first,
    each with its severity, why-it-matters, and the TAM's note.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prioritized roadmap">
    The promoted [Technology Roadmap](/composer/technology-roadmap) items, phased
    into Now / Next / Later, each carrying the Standard and control it traces back
    to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accepted-risk register">
    The risks the client chose to **Accept Risk & Defer**, each with its
    justification and required re-review date.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sign-off block">
    The TAM, vCIO, and client sign-offs — with the **captured signatures** rendered
    in place, and dates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review history">
    The sign-off / flag / return / reopen trail, so the TAM ↔ vCIO round-trip is
    visible on the deliverable itself.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Live snapshot before completion

You don't have to wait until the review is signed to generate the report — it
renders as a **live snapshot** at any point. Sign-offs that haven't happened yet
show as **Pending** rather than being faked, so an in-progress report is always an
honest picture of where the review stands.

<Note>
  The narrative prose is composed from the review's own score and counts — it
  states what the review found and never invents specifics the data doesn't carry.
  The report is read-only; it sends nothing and generates no files server-side —
  the PDF is produced by your browser's print dialog.
</Note>
