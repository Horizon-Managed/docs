---
title: "Contract coverage & profitability"
description: "On a client's 360° view, see logged ticket time split into covered / billable-extra / non-contract, and track a contract's profitability against its value or included hours"
---

Every client's 360° detail view (`/crm/clients`, then open a client) includes a
**contract coverage** panel that answers two questions at a glance: *how is our
time on this client being absorbed by their contract?* and *is that contract
still profitable?*

## When to use it

- Check whether a managed-services contract is still profitable — covered time
  against contract value.
- Spot a client running up a lot of ad-hoc work that isn't under any contract —
  a signal to propose one.
- Understand the mix of covered, billable-extra, and non-contract effort before a
  QBR or a renewal conversation.

## The hours ring

The hours ring breaks the client's logged ticket time into three parts:

| Segment | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Covered** | Time absorbed by an active contract — typically the non-billable managed-services effort you've already agreed to deliver. |
| **Billable-Extra** | Contract-linked work billed *on top* of the contract — in-scope client, out-of-scope-of-the-flat-fee work. |
| **Non-Contract** | Ad-hoc work not tied to any contract — pure break-fix or project time with no coverage behind it. |

Toggle the window between **week**, **month**, and **year** to see the mix over
different horizons.

<Note>
  A large or growing **Non-Contract** slice is the signal to watch. It means
  you're delivering unmanaged work — a clear opening to propose a contract that
  turns that ad-hoc time into recurring revenue.
</Note>

## Contract profitability meter

Alongside the ring, the profitability meter tracks how much of the active
contract has been consumed: covered hours (or their cost) burned against the
contract's value or its included hours. The meter shows one of three states:

- **Healthy** — comfortably within the contract's value or included hours.
- **Warning** — approaching the limit; worth a look before it tips over.
- **Over budget** — covered effort has exceeded what the contract pays for; the
  engagement is running at a loss and needs attention.

The meter also carries an **MRR / ORR / NRR** tag so you can see at a glance what
kind of revenue the contract represents:

- **MRR** — core recurring managed services.
- **ORR** — other recurring revenue (SaaS resale, VoIP, hosted services).
- **NRR** — non-recurring revenue (projects, break-fix, hardware).

## The value story

Together, the ring and the meter turn logged time into a profitability signal:

- **Watch covered vs. contract value.** When covered effort climbs toward — or
  past — the contract's value, the contract is thinning out. Catch it in the
  **warning** band and you can right-size scope or price at renewal instead of
  discovering the loss after the fact.
- **Turn Non-Contract time into recurring revenue.** A client generating steady
  ad-hoc work without a contract is leaving money on the table for both of you.
  The Non-Contract segment quantifies exactly how much, so the case for a
  contract writes itself.
