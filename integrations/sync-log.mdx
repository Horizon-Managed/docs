description: "Every integration outcome — QuickBooks, recurring billing, distributor catalogues and Hudu — in one time-ordered list, opening on failures."
---

The Sync Log at **Operations → Sync Log** shows every integration outcome across
the product in one place: QuickBooks pushes, recurring contract billing,
distributor catalogue syncs, and Hudu. Successes and failures both, ordered by
time.

Before it, those four lived in four separate tables and only one of them had a
page at all.

## Why it opens on failures

The list is sorted **failures first**, not newest first. That is deliberate.

An integration that fails every hour produces far more successful rows than
failed ones, so a list sorted purely by time shows twenty successes on the first
screen and buries the thing you need to see. The older QuickBooks-only surface
worked exactly that way, which is how a failure repeating hourly since April
stayed invisible on the one page built to show it.

<Note>
  Recurring billing recorded **11,478 failed attempts over four and a half
  months** that nothing in the product would ever have displayed. The Sync Log
  exists because of that.
</Note>

## Repeat attempts collapse

A sync that fails the same way every hour is one problem, not thousands of them.
Repeated attempts against the same record with the same error group into a
single row showing:

- what failed
- when it started
- when it last happened
- how many times

Expand the row to see the individual attempts.

## Filtering

| Filter | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Source** | Narrow to QuickBooks, recurring billing, distributor catalogue, or Hudu. |
| **Status** | Switch between failures and successes. |
| **Date range** | Scope to a billing period or an incident window. |
| **Error text** | Find every occurrence of a particular error message. |

## It is read-only

There is no retry button and no clear button, on purpose. Re-running a sync
raises invoices, and that deserves its own decision on the record it affects
rather than a bulk action from a log. Fix the underlying cause, then re-run from
the contract, invoice or purchase order itself.

## Relationship to the QuickBooks panel

The QuickBooks panel in **Settings → Integrations** stays where it is and now
links onward to the Sync Log. Its own history list also shows failures first,
and if its query fails it says so rather than reporting "No sync history yet" —
which previously made a broken query and a quiet integration look identical.

## What you can learn from it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the default view">
    Failures, most recent first. If this is empty, no integration has failed in
    the selected range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand a collapsed row">
    A high repeat count means something has been failing unattended. The "when
    it started" timestamp tells you how long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the error text">
    Integration failures are recorded with the underlying error rather than a
    generic message. A QuickBooks authentication failure and a missing contract
    line read differently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix and re-run at the source">
    Correct the cause, then re-run from the record — the contract, invoice or
    purchase order — not from the log.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Hours that could not be priced — a ticket time entry with no work type or
  billing role — are recorded here as a named Integration Failure when a
  QuickBooks push leaves them behind, instead of being dropped silently. See
  [time tracking](/time/index) for how to stop producing them.
</Tip>
