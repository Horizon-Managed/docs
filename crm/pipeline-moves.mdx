---
title: "Pipeline stage moves"
description: "Drag deals between stages and reorder cards to advance, win, or lose them — closing a deal is a single drop."
---

Your sales pipeline is a kanban board: one column per deal stage, one card per deal. You move a deal forward by dragging its card from one stage to the next — no form, no edit screen. Dropping a card into a **Won** or **Lost** stage closes the deal, and the board keeps every count, total, and forecast in sync as you go.

Open it at `/crm/pipeline`.

## When to use it

- Advancing a deal to the next stage as the opportunity progresses.
- Marking a deal **Won** or **Lost** the moment you know the outcome.
- Reordering the cards inside a stage so your most important deals sit at the top.
- Reopening a deal that was closed by mistake or that came back to life.
- Keeping your pipeline totals and forecast current without opening each deal.

## Move a deal between stages

Every card has a **drag handle** — the six-dot grip in its top-left corner. Dragging starts from the handle, so tapping or clicking the rest of the card never moves it by accident.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grab the handle">
    Press and hold the six-dot handle on the card you want to move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag to the target stage">
    Drag the card into another stage's column, or up and down within its current column to reorder it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop it">
    Release. The move saves immediately and the board refreshes so stage counts, per-stage totals, and the weighted forecast reflect the new position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reordering cards **within** a stage saves silently — there's no prompt, and nothing about the deal changes except its position in the column.

<Note>
  Only the drag handle starts a drag. Clicking the card body opens the deal's linked quote when it has one, and clicking the company name or the **Bid** / **Assessment** buttons follows those links instead.
</Note>

## Close a deal — Won or Lost

Some stages are flagged as closing stages: your **Won** stage and your **Lost** stage. Dragging a deal into one of these from any other stage is how you close it.

When a card crosses into a Won or Lost stage, NEXOS CORE asks you to confirm:

> Move this deal to "Won"? This closes the deal.

- **Confirm** — the move saves and the deal is stamped with a **close date** of the moment you dropped it.
- **Cancel** — the card snaps back to where it started and nothing changes.

The close date is what feeds your closed-revenue and win-rate reporting, so the confirm step protects you from closing a deal with a stray drag.

<Note>
  If a deal already has a close date — for example, you're just reordering cards **inside** your Won column — that original date is kept. Rearranging closed deals never resets anyone's close date to today.
</Note>

## Reopen a closed deal

Changed your mind, or a "lost" deal came back? Drag the card out of the Won or Lost stage and back into any open stage. There's no confirmation for this direction — the card simply returns to your open pipeline.

Once it's back in an open stage, the deal stops counting toward your won and lost figures (see below), because those metrics only include deals that are sitting in a closing stage.

## What updates automatically

Every move re-reads the board, so the numbers at the top of the page stay honest without a manual refresh:

| Metric | What it reflects |
|---|---|
| **Total Pipeline** | Combined value of every deal currently on the board, across all stages. |
| **Weighted Forecast** | Pipeline value weighted by each deal's win probability (value × probability). |
| **Active Deals** | Count of deals on the board. |
| **Avg Deal Size** | Total Pipeline divided by the number of deals on the board. |
| **Win Rate** | Won ÷ (won + lost) for deals closed in the **last 90 days**. |
| **Avg Days to Close** | Average time from creation to close for **won** deals in the last 90 days. |
| **Closed $** | Won-deal value by close date — this week, this month, each quarter, and year-to-date, each with a change chip versus the prior like-period. |

<Note>
  Win Rate and Avg Days to Close only consider deals closed in the trailing **90 days**, so both reflect your recent performance rather than your all-time history. A deal you reopen drops out of these figures because it's no longer in a closing stage.
</Note>

## How it connects

- Each card links straight to its **client record** and, when a quote is attached, opens that quote in one click — see [CRM](/crm/index).
- The **Bid** and **Assessment** shortcuts on a card start a new bid or site assessment pre-linked to that deal and company.
- Closing revenue and stage weighting build on your revenue classification — see [contract coverage & profitability](/crm/contract-coverage) for how MRR, ORR, and NRR are tracked across the account.
