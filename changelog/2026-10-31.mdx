Releases v1.102.0 to v1.115.0, from October 12 to October 29. This is the
biggest month so far. Every invoice your client receives now goes through
QuickBooks first, project billing was rebuilt from start to finish, and the
ticket and RMM screens were redesigned. Several changes alter how billing works
day to day, so read the warnings below.

## Billing and QuickBooks

<Warning>
  **Recurring billing now comes only from QuickBooks.** NEXOS CORE's own hourly
  recurring-billing run is gone. QuickBooks recurring templates do all recurring
  billing. A live contract with no QuickBooks template says **Not billing: no
  QuickBooks recurring template** on its page. The **Recurring contract
  billing** switch has been removed from Integrations.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **QuickBooks no longer decides which client a customer belongs to.** The
  QuickBooks sync used to link customers to clients by name, create a client for
  every customer it couldn't match, and overwrite clients' email and phone. It
  now only refreshes the list of QuickBooks customers. A person links each
  client to its QuickBooks customer, and every link is audited.
</Warning>

- **QuickBooks sends your invoices.** Push a draft invoice with **Push to
  QuickBooks**, then use **Send from QuickBooks** to email it. NEXOS CORE no
  longer emails invoices itself. Invoices show **In QuickBooks — not sent**
  until QuickBooks sends them. The invoice list adds a **Not sent** filter and
  bulk send. See [Quotes & invoices](/billing/index).
- **QuickBooks' figures win.** An invoice in QuickBooks shows QuickBooks'
  subtotal, tax, total and balance, and can only be edited in QuickBooks.
  **Record Payment** posts the payment to QuickBooks first. Credit memos are
  issued in QuickBooks and appear on the invoice they were applied to, so
  **Issue Credit** has been removed. Voiding an invoice voids it in QuickBooks
  and frees the work it billed so it can be billed again.
- **Ready to bill** (Billing → Ready to bill) is billing's queue across every
  client: completed project milestones, final invoices, signed change orders,
  approved expenses, time-and-materials hours, ticket time and drafts that never
  reached QuickBooks. Rows that can't be billed yet say why and link to the fix.
  **Create in QuickBooks** previews every invoice before it is created. The
  queue is also on your phone, under More.
- **Ticket time is reviewed before it bills.** A support admin approves each
  closed ticket's billable time. They can trim the hours that bill, exclude an
  entry with a reason, send time back to the technician, and word the line the
  client will read. The client's invoice shows one line per ticket, with no
  hours or rates. Work the client's contract covers can never be marked
  billable.
- **Clients and QuickBooks customers.** New Client checks QuickBooks for
  matching customers before the client is created. You can link one, or create
  the client in both places. Every client page shows which QuickBooks customer
  it bills, and a new **Finance** tab mirrors its balance, invoices, payments
  and credit memos. Choose sandbox or production per tenant under **Settings →
  QuickBooks**. See [QuickBooks](/integrations/quickbooks).
- **Recurring templates bill the right lines.** A contract's QuickBooks template
  bills its recurring lines only, so one-time setup fees are no longer billed
  every month. When a new contract version is signed after a seat change, it
  replaces the old version's template instead of billing alongside it.
- **Default contracts.** You can mark a client's default service-desk contract
  on the contract page. New tickets use the default. If no default is set and
  the client has exactly one contract in force, they use that contract;
  otherwise the ticket is Time & Materials.
- **Fewer double counts.** QuickBooks product sync no longer marks every Service
  item as a monthly charge. Approved timesheet hours go to QuickBooks as
  not-billable time, so they aren't offered for invoicing twice. Survey time is
  never billable.

## Projects

<Warning>
  **Only people with billing access create project invoices.** Completing a
  milestone no longer raises an invoice by itself: it marks the milestone
  **Ready to bill**, and billing raises the invoice. The same applies to change
  orders, expenses and final invoices. Nobody can approve an expense they
  entered.
</Warning>

- **A Billing tab on every project** answers "what is left to bill on this
  job?". It shows the contract value, change orders, the amount in QuickBooks,
  the amount ready to bill and the remainder. Below that is one row per billable
  item, with its state and any blocker. A **How this project bills** card on the
  project page lists what must be in place before billing can reach QuickBooks.
- **New projects ask how they bill**: fixed price, milestones,
  time-and-materials (with an optional not-to-exceed), or not billable. Budget
  lines carry a client price beside their cost, and every tab shows the same
  figures. A signed contract creates its project from a preview of exactly what
  will be created. See [Projects](/project/index).
- **Change orders are priced from lines** (labour, materials and fixed fees),
  with a running price, expected cost and margin. You decide when each one
  bills: on approval, with a milestone, or at completion. The client sees prices
  only. On the signing page they can approve, decline with a reason, or ask a
  question, and you can hand your phone to the client to sign.
- **Milestones and time-and-materials.** Milestones can be entered as an amount
  or a percentage, show how many days late they are, and ask you to confirm
  before completing. Time-and-materials projects bill month after month, and no
  hour is billed twice.
- **Expenses and materials.** Expenses carry a markup and a receipt, and you can
  log them from your phone. Materials received on a billable purchase order are
  billed on the project's next charges invoice. Job Costing and Profitability
  forecast revenue from what the project actually bills.
- **Tasks are an outline.** Drag to reorder and nest tasks, and use Tab and
  Shift+Tab to indent and outdent. WBS numbers follow each task's position.
  Parent tasks roll up hours, dates and progress. Tasks can be assigned and
  timed from the desktop.

## Helpdesk

- **The ticket page is rebuilt.** One chronological stream replaces the
  Conversation and Activity tabs, with tabs to filter it. **Actions ▸ Edit
  ticket** edits the title, status, priority, team, assignee, type, source,
  contact, contract and company. A ticket's value shows total labour, with the
  billable and contract-covered amounts beneath it.
- **The ticket queue sorts on every column.** Contact has its own column,
  Priority is a column again, the column header stays in place while you scroll,
  and every column can be resized. The lane headings are gone, so your sort
  applies to the whole list. See [Ticket queue](/helpdesk/ticket-queue).
- **An SLA runway** replaces the queue's stat tiles. It places every open ticket
  by the time left until its next deadline, with counts for past due, due within
  the hour, due in one to two hours, paused and no SLA. Each count opens a
  filtered queue.
- **Pending pauses the SLA clock.** Time a ticket spends in Pending, or in any
  status you've marked as pausing the SLA, is added back to its deadline. Paused
  tickets show **Paused**. The clock stops when a ticket is resolved or closed,
  and a ticket resolved on time shows **✓ Met**.
- **Honest labels.** Tickets a technician creates are recorded as **Manual**,
  and onboarding and email security tickets no longer show **PORTAL**.
  Automation is shown as **Nexie (Automation)** instead of "Unknown" or
  "System". Related tickets say why they're related and which device they're
  on.

## RMM

- **A new Devices list** under a reorganised RMM menu (Fleet, Operate,
  Configure; old links still work). It has a fleet health strip, search by
  hostname, user, IP, MAC or serial, filters, saved views, a side drawer and
  bulk ticket creation. See [Devices](/rmm/devices).
- **The device page is rebuilt.** It shows the business context (company,
  contract, contact, SLA and open tickets), eight tabs from Overview to
  Services, and device **notes** and **custom fields**. See
  [Device details](/rmm/device-details).
- **The RMM Dashboard shows fleet metrics**, with a client picker. See
  [RMM dashboard](/rmm/dashboard).
- **Alerts can raise tickets.** Turn on **Raise tickets from RMM alerts** under
  **Settings → General → Agent Staleness Detection**. A server that goes offline
  then opens a ticket for the right client. If the device comes back before
  anyone works the ticket, the ticket resolves itself with a plain-English
  note. See [Alert tickets](/helpdesk/alert-tickets).
- **Deploy by company.** Every company gets its enrolment token when it is
  created, and the Deploy page shows each company's download, share link and
  install command. The Windows install command now works. See
  [Deploy agent](/rmm/deploy-agent).
- **Better, safer agents.** Agents report their version and real CPU, memory
  and disk figures. Windows software inventory is correct, the Windows service
  restarts after a crash, and uninstalling an agent removes it cleanly. Each
  agent can only report for itself, and remote sessions open only on your
  organization's devices.

## Products, distributors and quotes

- **The product catalog scales.** It pages, sorts and filters on the server,
  with saved views, a quick look per product and bulk edit. A product can be
  **Priced per quote**. See [Product catalog](/product/index).
- **Distributors moved to Settings → Integrations.** Each sync run has its own
  page with errors grouped by problem. **Match clients** links Pax8 and Sherweb
  companies to your clients, and Sherweb now connects directly. Each client's
  Subscriptions tab groups subscriptions by distributor, and **Link to
  contract** bills the catalog price, never the distributor's cost.
- **Quotes.** **Get approval link** on a draft quote gives you a link to share
  yourself, without sending an email. The Nexie Quote Builder has a microphone
  for dictation. In Chrome and Edge the audio is transcribed by the browser
  vendor; Safari transcribes it on the device.

## Bug fixes

- Names sort alphabetically regardless of capital letters.
- Creating and editing contracts works again after the "priced per quote"
  change.
- Dashboard tiles for SLA breaches, online agents and companies show real
  numbers.
- Filter menus no longer pop open on their own, and sheets and dialogs keep
  focus until you close them.
- Peek's keyboard shortcuts no longer fire while you're typing a reply.
