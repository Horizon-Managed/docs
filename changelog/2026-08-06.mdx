title: "Week of August 6, 2026"
description: "Sales Workspace dates now save, deal stage history is recorded again, close-won automation actually closes the deal, and admin-only notifications stop going to everyone."
---

Four fixes, all in the sales and automation paths: the Sales Workspace date
fields save, a deal's stage history is recorded again, an automation rule that
closes a deal as won now really closes it, and a notification meant for admins
no longer reaches every user.

## Sales Workspace date fields now save

On the **NexOS C²** panel, **Close Date**, **Last Step Date**, **Next Step
Date** and **Entered PBR** did not save. Clicking a day in the calendar looked
like it worked — the field showed your choice — but the value never reached the
deal, so reloading the page or opening the deal elsewhere showed the old date
(or none at all).

All four now save the moment you pick a day, with no separate save step, and the
change streams to the **Sales Cockpit** like every other field on the panel.

Two related improvements landed with it:

- **Dates are calendar days.** A date you pick reads back as exactly that day
  for everyone who opens the deal, regardless of their time zone. Previously a
  stored date could render as the day before the one that was chosen.
- **The calendar re-opens on the stored date.** Click into a field that already
  has a date and the calendar lands on that month and day, so you can shift a
  close date by a week without hunting for it.

If you have been keeping these dates somewhere else — a note on the deal, a
spreadsheet, the deal title — you can move them onto the fields now. Dates that
had been entered before this fix were never stored, so the fields will read
empty until you set them; nothing needs to be corrected or cleaned up.

See [Sales Workspace → Stage & timeline](/crm/sales-workspace#stage--timeline)
for how the fields work.

## Deal stage history is recorded again

A deal's history — the entries in the **activity feed** on the Sales Workspace
and on the deal's timeline — had stopped capturing stage changes, including the
move to **won** or **lost**. Other activity (quotes, emails, meetings, timer
starts) kept recording normally, so the omission was easy to miss until you went
looking for when a deal actually advanced.

Stage changes are recorded again, whether you drag the card on the
[pipeline board](/crm/pipeline-moves) or an automation rule moves the deal for
you. History from the period when records were being dropped cannot be
reconstructed, so timelines will show a gap before this release.

## "Close deal as won" automation now closes the deal

An automation rule using the **close deal as won** action reported that it ran
and recorded a success in its action log, but the deal never left its current
stage. Anyone relying on the rule had to move the deal by hand while the log
insisted the automation had done it.

The action now moves the deal.

<Warning>
  If you have a rule with the **close deal as won** action enabled, it starts
  taking effect on the next matching event. Deals that would have been closed
  silently over the last stretch will now genuinely move to **Closed Won** —
  which shifts your won totals, win rate and forecast. Worth reviewing that
  rule's conditions before your next matching event if you built it a while ago
  and never saw it fire.
</Warning>

## Admin-only notifications stop going to everyone

A workflow's **send notification** action set to deliver to **admins** was
delivering to every user in the tenant instead. Anything you routed to admins —
approval nudges, escalations, internal-only warnings — reached your whole staff.

Admin-targeted notifications now go only to users holding your tenant's admin
role. Two related cases changed with it:

- If your tenant has **no admin role defined**, the notification is not sent at
  all rather than broadcast to everyone. A missed internal notification is
  recoverable; telling all staff something meant for admins is not.
- An **unrecognized recipient setting** no longer falls back to a tenant-wide
  broadcast.

If you built a rule around the old behavior — using **admins** because it
happened to reach everyone — switch it to **all users**, which delivers to your
whole staff by design.

## Bug fixes

- Sales Workspace **Close Date**, **Last Step Date**, **Next Step Date** and
  **Entered PBR** not saving (as above)
- Sales Workspace date fields rendering a day earlier than the day picked
- Deal stage changes, including won and lost, missing from a deal's history
  (as above)
- Automation **close deal as won** action reporting success without moving the
  deal (as above)
- Workflow **send notification** to **admins** delivering to every user
  (as above)
- Group notifications — all users, a role, or admins — triggered from an
  in-app action occasionally delivering to nobody while reporting success.
  Affected the assessment-approval and quote-generated notifications.
- The **Helpdesk Configuration** settings page loading its status, priority,
  team, type, category, source, impact and urgency lists more slowly than it
  needed to on first paint.
- A malformed date sent to the Sales Workspace now returns a clean validation
  error instead of an unhelpful server error.
