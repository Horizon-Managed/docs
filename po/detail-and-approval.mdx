Every purchase order opens to a full detail page at `/purchase-orders/{id}` —
the canonical surface for reviewing a PO and acting on it. This is where an
approver sees exactly what is being requested, from whom, and against which
client or work, before releasing the spend.

## When to use it

- Review the complete contents of a PO before approving or rejecting it.
- Approve, reject, or receive a purchase order.
- See who requested a PO, who approved it, and when each step happened.
- Confirm a PO pushed to QuickBooks, and trace it back to the ticket or project
  it was raised for.

## The detail page

The detail page is organized into a header, the line items, the money summary,
and notes.

### Header

The header carries the PO's identity and status at a glance:

| Field | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **PO number** | The auto-assigned `PO-YYYY-NNN` identifier. |
| **Status** | Current lifecycle state (draft, pending approval, approved, rejected, received). |
| **Priority** | The urgency set on the PO. |
| **Vendor** | The vendor the goods or services are being ordered from. |
| **Company** | The client the purchase is associated with. |
| **Requested by / Approved by** | The user who raised the PO and the approver who released it. |
| **Created / Approved / Received dates** | The timestamp for each lifecycle step, as it happens. |
| **QuickBooks PO number** | Shown once the approved PO has pushed to QuickBooks. |
| **Linked ticket / project** | The ticket or project the PO was raised against, if any. |

### Line items

A line-items table lists each product or service on the order — **product**,
**description**, **quantity**, **unit price**, and **line total**.

### Totals

Below the line items, the money summary shows **subtotal**, **tax**,
**shipping**, and the **order total**.

### Notes and rejection reason

Free-text **notes** carry any context added to the PO. If a PO was rejected, the
**rejection reason** is shown so the requester understands why and can raise a
corrected PO.

## Approval workflow

A new purchase order enters **pending approval** unless its total falls under the
tenant's configured auto-approve dollar threshold — in which case it approves
automatically on creation.

Approvers — users who hold the approve permission or who sit on the tenant's
approver list — can act on a PO from either the purchase-order list or the detail
page:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Release the PO for fulfillment. Approval captures the approver's
    **signature**, stamps the approved-by user and the approval date, and (for
    distributor SaaS) makes the PO eligible to dispatch. An approved PO can push
    to QuickBooks as a QBO purchase order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reject">
    Decline the PO with a **reason**. The reason is recorded on the detail page so
    the requester knows what to fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive">
    Mark the goods or services as received once they arrive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When a PO needs approval, its approvers receive a **notification that deep-links
straight to that PO**, so releasing spend is a single click from the alert.

<Note>
  **Approvers and the auto-approve limit are managed under Settings →
  Procurement.** That is where you set the dollar threshold below which POs
  approve automatically, and where you maintain the list of who can approve.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Approval is not provisioning.** For distributor SaaS, approving a PO makes it
  eligible to dispatch but does not send it to the distributor — see
  [Purchase orders & procurement](/po/index) for the dispatch and remediation
  flow.
</Note>
