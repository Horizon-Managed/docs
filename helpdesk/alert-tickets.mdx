---
title: "Tickets raised by RMM alerts"
description: "How an RMM alert opens a ticket, resolves it when the alert clears, and what the ticket page shows about the alert."
---

NexOS can open a ticket when an RMM alert fires on a managed device and resolve that ticket when the alert clears. Today the alert that does this is **Device offline**. By default it raises tickets for servers only, at **high** priority.

## Turn it on

In **Settings → Tenant → Agent Staleness Detection**:

- **Raise tickets from RMM alerts** turns the feature on for the tenant. It is off by default.
- **Email the client about tickets raised by RMM alerts** decides whether workflow emails reach the device's contact and the client's company address. When it is off, technicians are still notified, and a reply you write to the contact still sends.

A device counts as offline after the tenant's offline threshold, set in the same section. A device that has been silent for more than 7 days is treated as retired and raises nothing.

## What happens to the ticket

- **The alert fires.** A ticket opens for the device's client with the device linked and its assigned contact on the ticket.
- **The alert clears, and no one has worked the ticket.** The ticket resolves itself. The bridge posts a note saying the device is back and uses it as the resolution.
- **The alert clears, and someone has worked the ticket.** The ticket stays open, and the bridge posts one note saying the device is back.
- **The device keeps flapping.** If the alert has fired 3 times in 24 hours, the ticket stays open for a person to look at.
- **Many devices go down at once.** After 20 alert tickets for one client in an hour, NexOS opens one roll-up ticket for the rest instead of one ticket per device.

## The Raised by alert card

A ticket an alert raised shows a **Raised by alert** card on the ticket page, under **Linked asset**. Tickets made by hand don't have one.

| Row | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Firing / Cleared** | Whether the alert is still firing. |
| **Alert** | The alert's name, for example *Device offline*. |
| **Device** | The device it fired on. If you can read devices, it links to the device's **Alerts and Activity** tab. |
| **First seen** | When the alert started. |
| **Last seen** | While firing, when it was last reported. |
| **Cleared** | When it cleared and how long it fired. |
| **Observations** | How many times the alert was observed while it fired. |
| **Fired before** | The ticket the same alert raised the previous time it fired on this device. |

When the alert has cleared, the card also says whether the ticket **resolved automatically** or was **left open for a person**. The note posted when it cleared says why it was left open.

A **roll-up** ticket's card says it stands for many devices. Each device lists its own alert on its **Alerts and Activity** tab ([device details](/rmm/device-details)).

If the device has since been uninstalled, its alert history goes with it. The card then just says an RMM alert raised the ticket.
