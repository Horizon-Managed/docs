An alignment review is finished **with the client**. After the internal gates are
done — the TAM has judged the controls and signed off, and the vCIO has
countersigned — NEXOS CORE hands the review to the client on their own device to
walk the findings and sign. The client sign-off is the **only** way an alignment
review reaches complete.

It's the same live-review-and-sign flow the
[compliance review](/composer/ncsr/index) uses, so it behaves identically to the
compliance walkthrough.

## Where the walkthrough sits in the gated flow

The [alignment review](/composer/alignment-reviews) runs through an ordered chain,
and the client walkthrough is the last two gates:

1. **Judge controls** — the TAM assesses each control (aligned / at-risk /
   not-aligned / N/A).
2. **Build recommendations** — every misaligned finding is dispositioned: promoted
   to the roadmap or **Accept Risk & Defer**.
3. **TAM sign-off** — the TAM signs off the technical assessment, which freezes it.
4. **vCIO countersign** — the vCIO reviews and countersigns (or returns it to the
   TAM).
5. **Client walkthrough** — the client walks the findings on their own device.
6. **Client sign-off** — the client signs, sealing the review as complete.

The walkthrough handoff **auto-surfaces** the moment the review reaches this stage
— the QR is ready and lit as the active step — so there's no separate setup.

## The walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hand off the review">
    From the review, the walkthrough handoff is already waiting: a **QR code** plus
    a **link**. NEXOS CORE reuses the review's live, short-lived token (24 hours)
    rather than minting a new one on every refresh. The client scans the QR on an
    iPad, or you send them the link to open on a laptop or any browser — the
    walkthrough is a standard web page and runs anywhere. No login: the link
    authenticates the client to that one review only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client walks the findings">
    The client sees each control alongside the TAM's alignment judgment,
    **read-only** — they review and acknowledge the assessment but cannot change
    it. The findings, the roadmap dispositions, and any accepted-risk deferrals are
    all there to see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign off">
    At the end the client applies an **on-device e-signature** and signs off the
    whole review, sealing it and finalizing every accepted risk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signed deliverable">
    The completed review produces the branded
    [Executive Summary](/composer/executive-summary) — the alignment score,
    findings, roadmap, accepted-risk register, and the sign-off block with every
    captured signature — and the whole exchange is written to a tenant-scoped audit
    trail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The handoff token is short-lived (24h) and scoped to a single review; the link
  works on any device, so the client can complete the walkthrough on an iPad,
  laptop, or phone. The TAM's judgments are never editable by the client.
</Note>
