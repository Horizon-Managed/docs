Every technician has **one running timer at a time**. While it runs, a bar
across the top of every page shows which ticket you're on, how long you've
been on it, and the controls to pause, stop or discard it. When you open that
ticket, the bar steps aside and the same clock sits in the ticket's header
instead.

By default, opening a ticket starts its timer for you. You can turn that off
per user.

## When to use it

- Track time on a ticket without remembering to press start.
- See at a glance, from any page, which ticket your clock is on.
- Jump back to the ticket you're timing with one click.
- Switch tickets without leaving two clocks running.
- Know when a ticket is running past its escalation time.

## The timer bar

The bar sits under the app header on every page except the ticket it's timing.
From left to right it shows:

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Status dot | A live dot while running; a pause icon while paused. |
| Ticket | The ticket number, title and client. Click it to open the ticket. |
| Note | A short note about what you're doing. Click **Add a note** (or the note itself) to edit it. The clock keeps running while you type; **Enter** saves, **Esc** cancels. |
| State | **Running**, **Paused**, or **Past _N_ min** once it crosses a threshold (see [colours](#how-the-bar-changes-colour)). |
| Elapsed | Hours, minutes and seconds on this timer. |

And the actions:

- **Pause** / **Resume**.
- **Stop & log** — stops the clock and opens the log form, where you confirm
  the work type, billing role and contract. See
  [Time tracking](/time/index#from-timer-to-bill).
- **Escalate** — appears once a running timer goes past the critical threshold.
  It lists the ticket's SLA escalation tiers; pick one to escalate to it. With
  no tiers configured, it offers **Mark as escalated**.
- **Discard** (bin icon) — throws the timer away and logs nothing. You get a
  few seconds to **Undo**.
- **+_N_ paused** — appears when you have paused timers on other tickets. It
  opens a list where you can open, resume, stop or discard each one.

## On the ticket itself

When you open the ticket your timer is on, the bar hides and a **timer chip**
appears in the ticket header in place of the **Start timer** button. The chip
shows the elapsed time with pause/resume and stop buttons, and changes colour
the same way the bar does. Leave the ticket and the bar comes back.

On a ticket with no timer, the header shows **Start timer**.

## One timer runs at a time

Starting or resuming a timer **pauses whichever one was running**. Paused timers
keep their elapsed time and wait in the **+_N_ paused** list until you resume,
stop or discard them. Timers are kept on the server, so the same timer follows
you between desktop and the [mobile app](/mobile/index).

## Auto-start when you open a ticket

With auto-start on, opening a ticket in **full view** starts its timer (or
resumes its paused one), and pauses the timer you were running before.

Auto-start does **not** fire when:

- you open the ticket in the [Peek](/helpdesk/ticket-peek) — peeking at a
  ticket never starts a clock;
- the ticket is closed — reading a closed ticket's history doesn't start
  billing;
- that ticket's timer is already running.

### Turn it on or off

The switch is in the ticket's full view, under the header:
**Start a timer when I open a ticket**. It's per user, on by default, and
applies everywhere you sign in.

If you untick it while this ticket's timer is running, that timer is
**discarded** — you've just said you didn't want it. The usual **Undo** brings
it back. With auto-start off, press **Start timer** when you begin work.

## Under a minute is discarded

A timer that has run **less than one minute in total** is treated as a glance,
not work, so it is thrown away instead of being kept when:

- you **pause** it;
- you **switch** to another ticket, which would otherwise pause it;
- you **close** its ticket.

A notice reads **Under a minute — timer discarded. Nothing was logged.** For a
pause or a switch you get a few seconds to **Undo**; undoing a pause keeps the
timer, paused. Closing a ticket reloads the page, so that notice appears after
the reload with no Undo.

A timer that ran a minute or more is never discarded automatically. On a closed
ticket it stays until you stop and log it, or discard it yourself. **Stop &
log** always logs, however short the timer.

<Note>
The under-a-minute discard happens in the web app. Pausing or switching on the
[mobile app](/mobile/index) keeps short timers.
</Note>

## How the bar changes colour

The bar and the chip change colour as the timer runs:

| State | When |
|---|---|
| Normal | Under the warning threshold. |
| Warning (orange) | Past the warning threshold. |
| Danger (red) | Past the danger threshold. |
| Critical | Past the critical threshold. **Escalate** appears. |

The thresholds come from the ticket's SLA escalation tiers: tier 1 sets warning,
tier 2 danger and tier 3 critical. A ticket with fewer tiers fills the gaps 5
and 10 minutes after its highest tier. A ticket with no escalation tiers uses
your organisation's **Timer Escalation Thresholds** in
[tenant settings](/admin/tenant-settings), which default to 20, 25 and 30
minutes.

## Good to know

- **Discard logs nothing.** Use **Stop & log** for any time you want billed.
- **Undo only lasts a few seconds.** Once the notice goes, the discard is final.
- The bar shows your own timers only.
