title: "What clients see in the portal"
description: "The read-only Technology Alignment mirror in the client portal — a client's live alignment score, upcoming review, and roadmap, scoped to their own company"
---

Everything the TAM and vCIO build inside NexOS Summit —
[the alignment score](/composer/alignment-reviews),
[the technology roadmap](/composer/technology-roadmap) — has a **read-only mirror**
in the client's own portal. Your client logs into the
[Client Portal](/portal/index) and sees, on one page, how their environment
measures against the standard you assigned them and what's planned ahead. There's
nothing new for you to publish — the portal reads the same live data your team
maintains. Clients open it at `/portal/alignment`.

## What the client sees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Their alignment score">
    The live score from the client's latest non-cancelled alignment review, shown
    out of 100 with a progress meter and a breakdown of how many controls came
    back **Aligned**, **At risk**, and **Not aligned** (controls marked N/A are
    excluded from the score). It's the same score your team sees — the client is
    looking at the real assessment, not a snapshot. Before their first review is
    complete, the page shows a friendly "no alignment review yet" placeholder
    instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Their upcoming review">
    The next scheduled alignment review — its date, time, standard, and cadence —
    so the client always knows when their next checkpoint is. From here they can
    also [request a reschedule](/composer/reschedule-requests).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Their technology roadmap">
    The client's planned and in-flight initiatives, each with its status,
    priority, category, target quarter, and cost. Items promoted from an alignment
    review carry the same **provenance chip** your team sees, so the client can
    trace a roadmap item back to the standard and control that justified it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read-only by design

The portal alignment page is strictly **read-only**. There is no edit path — a
client can view their score and roadmap, but can never change a control judgment,
a roadmap item, or the score. The one action they can take is to
[request a reschedule](/composer/reschedule-requests) of their upcoming review,
which goes to your team's approval queue rather than changing anything directly.

The score and roadmap the client sees are computed from the exact same data and
scoring logic as the internal TAM/vCIO workspace, so the two surfaces can never
drift apart.

## Scoped to their company

Portal users are *contacts*, not MSP staff, and authenticate through the portal's
[passwordless magic-link stack](/portal/index#logging-in). Every session is bound
to a single tenant and company, and the alignment page only ever shows **that
company's** score, schedule, and roadmap. Which company's data a client sees is
resolved server-side from their session — never from anything in the request — so
one client can never see another's alignment.

<Note>
  The portal mirror is the client-facing counterpart to the internal
  [vCIO QBR](/composer/vcio-qbr): the QBR is the deliverable you present and
  print, while the portal is the always-on, self-service view the client can
  return to any time between reviews.
</Note>
