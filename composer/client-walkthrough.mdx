---

Once the TAM has completed the initial alignment review, the findings are reviewed
**with the client**. NEXOS CORE hands the review to the client on their own device —
an iPad via QR code, or any browser via a link — where they walk each finding,
accept the risks they've chosen not to remediate, and sign off. It's the same
live-review-and-sign flow the [compliance review](/composer/ncsr/index) uses, so
it behaves identically to the compliance walkthrough.

## Two passes

The alignment review is intentionally two passes:

1. **TAM review** — the technician judges each control (aligned / at-risk /
   not-aligned / N/A). This is the technical assessment; the client isn't involved.
2. **Client walkthrough** — you sit with the client and walk the findings. This is
   where risk acceptance and the signature happen.

## The walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hand off the review">
    From the review, choose **Begin client walkthrough**. NEXOS CORE mints a
    short-lived token (24 hours) and shows a **QR code** plus a **link**. The
    client scans the QR on an iPad, or you send them the link to open on a laptop
    or any browser — the walkthrough is a standard web page and runs anywhere. No
    login: the link authenticates the client to that one review only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client walks the findings">
    The client sees each control alongside the TAM's alignment judgment,
    **read-only** — the client reviews and acknowledges the assessment but cannot
    change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept risks">
    On any **not-aligned** or **at-risk** finding the client won't remediate, they
    **accept the risk** — capturing their **initials**, a short **reason**, and a
    required **re-review date**. Accepting a risk is a decision, not a passing
    grade: it doesn't change the alignment score, and the risk resurfaces for
    re-review on the date chosen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign off">
    At the end the client applies an **on-device e-signature** and signs off the
    whole review, sealing it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signed binder">
    Sign-off produces a **signed PDF binder** — the alignment score, findings by
    state, the accepted-risk register (with re-review dates), and the signature —
    and writes the whole exchange to a tenant-scoped audit trail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The handoff token is short-lived (24h) and scoped to a single review; the link
  works on any device, so the client can complete the walkthrough on an iPad,
  laptop, or phone. The TAM's judgments are never editable by the client.
</Note>
